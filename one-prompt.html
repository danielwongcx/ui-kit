Eight studio pieces, what fails without each one, and what to do instead.
Piece Without it Fix
Frame function A small edit means painting the whole film again. Paint any second on its own.
The rig The model never sees or hears the film. An agent, a browser, and ffmpeg.
The brief Safe defaults fill every open choice. Write the production doc.
The reference A centered title lands on a gradient. Borrow a look. Keep your own subject.
State lists Effects, with no story holding them. One shape. Each beat has a job.
Springs & sound Motion feels weightless, and the sound drifts. Give the motion weight. Use one clock.
The critic The first pass is the one that ships. Score the sheet. Fix the worst three. Continue until every score is 8 or higher.
The skill Every film starts from an empty folder. The next film is one command.
