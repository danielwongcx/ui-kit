Rules proximity
  • One spacing scale. Don't freestyle px.
  • More space around a group than inside it. Proximity is relationship.
  • Start with generous whitespace, then tighten.
  • Macro gaps between sections. Tighter inside cards and forms.
  • Don't use 8px everywhere. Jump at section level.

HK life-sim uses an 8px grid, 16px page pad, 8px chrome gaps, and taps of at least 44. Same 8px language, product-specific.

Grids fixed · max-width

Give the sidebar a fixed width sized for its labels. The main column takes the rest, and can keep its own grid inside.

The mid frame is narrower, and its column-count card is wider. Max-width holds the card until the frame is smaller than the card.

Relative size type · padding

On a small screen the body goes to 14px. A 2.5em headline stays 35px. A headline with its own size can sit at 22px.

Em padding zooms one button. Tuned padding is tighter on the small one and more generous on the large one.

Groups around, then within

When space is what connects a group, leave more of it around the group than inside. Same for a heading, a list, and a horizontal cluster.
