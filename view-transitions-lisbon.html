Lisbon

12 min read

The hill keeps its lamps on after the kitchens close. From a miradouro the river is a dark band, and the bridge draws one thin line across it.

Walk down through Alfama and the streets turn into stairs. Stand still when the tram takes the corner. The pastel that is still warm is the one worth the wait.

Kyoto

12 min read

After the shutters come down, the lane is wood, shadow, and the last warm light in a paper window.

A gate holds the street for a moment. Follow the river path between the two bridges and the city gets quiet without going empty.

Oaxaca

12 min read

The courtyard stays warm after dinner. Smoke hangs low, and the mountains cut a hard edge out of the sky.

This walk stays with the evening: stalls packing up, a quieter block, and the long way back to the zócalo.

Tromso

12 min read

Harbour lights go green when the aurora shows, and plain white when it is only snow. The sun does not come up today.

The walk is short: from the quay to a window that faces north, then back before the wind picks up.

Hanoi

12 min read

The lake holds a still moon. Stools stack, then a few come out again. The motorbikes thin to a line you can cross.

Take the long way around the water after ten. The old quarter is louder on the far side and calm by the trees.
