How to use this list

  1. 1

    Spend fifteen minutes with one site and leave the other tabs closed.

  2. 2

    When a page earns a save, name the reason in one sentence: the space, the contrast in the type, a grey, or the easing.

  3. 3

    Pick one piece you keep returning to, step through it in the inspector or frame by frame, and rebuild a single part yourself.

  4. 4

    When you ask an assistant for a direction, point it at a style, a file, and a person. “Modern” and “premium” leave it nothing to aim at.
