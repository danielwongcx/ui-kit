Tick rail

Ticks grow around the pointer. One card previews the section under it.

The quay

The night boat ties up at the north end, beside the green lamp. Crates wait in a line the clerk can count without a lantern.

The lamp

It stays lit from dusk until the last ramp comes up. If it flickers, the desk has a spare chimney in the left drawer.

The ledger

Names go in the right-hand column. The left column is the lamp number, written once and never crossed out.

The ramp

It is down for ten minutes at each crossing. The lower steps are wet when the tide is out, so a rope runs along the rail.

The locker

Brass tags hang on the east wall. Bring the tag to the desk before noon if a crate has to move.

The night boat

It leaves at 21:40 unless the harbour master holds it. The clerk calls the names once, then the ramp comes up.
