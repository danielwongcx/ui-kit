Six studio pieces, what fails without each one, and what to do instead.
Piece Without it Fix
Reference Centered type on a gradient. Hand it a frame. Keep your subject.
Spec A mood, and nothing to check it against. Write the states, in order.
Engine The model cannot write an MP4. Paint any moment from the clock, then render.
Springs The move eases, then stops dead. Give it mass. One spring per change.
Sound Music glued on after the picture. Cuts and effects on one beat grid.
Critique The first pass is the one you post. Contact sheet. Worst three. Scores of 8+.
