03 Invariant architectural and spatial laws

Ten constraints. Break one and the group, the column, or the motion stops reading as a single object.

01 Inner ≤ outer 02 Buttons 03 Expansion 04 Radii 05 Flex and grid 06 min-width 07 Sticky flex 08 Drawer 09 Ballistics 10 Path parity

Law 1 · Inner ≤ outer

The gap inside a container stays at or under the container’s padding, so the frame still reads as one group.

Gapinternal ≤ Paddingcontainer

Law 2 · Asymmetrical buttons

Horizontal padding runs about two to three times the vertical padding. A label is wide and short; equal padding makes the control look square.

Paddinghorizontal = 2× to 3× Paddingvertical

Law 3 · Expansion first

Start at 1.5rem to 2rem. Tighten toward 1rem only when a cluster starts to feel disconnected. Cramped type fails sooner than open space.

Law 4 · Concentric radii

The inner radius is the outer radius minus the padding, and it never drops below zero. Nested corners then share a center.

Radiusinner = max(0, Radiusouter − Padding)

Law 5 · Flex for one axis, grid for two

Flexbox is the default for a toolbar, a row, or any cluster whose children size themselves. Grid is for a two-dimensional frame where the parent places every child.

Law 6 · flex-basis and min-width: 0

Equal columns use flex: 1 1 0% and min-width: 0. Without both, a long token sets the column minimum and the row overflows.

Law 7 · Sticky flex anchoring

A sticky item in a flex row needs align-self: flex-start. Stretch makes it as tall as the row, so the links travel with the scroll instead of pinning.

Law 8 · Off-canvas escape

On a small screen the drawer is position: absolute inside a relative frame. On a wide screen it returns to normal flow. Open each one.

Law 9 · Non-destructive ballistics

A throw across the screen clones the element, flies the clone, and deletes the clone on finish. The original stays in the grid. The counter changes when the flight ends.

Law 10 · Path parity

Two paths can morph when they share the same number and order of commands. Normalize the vertices first. A mismatched pair pops.

04 Dual-pass execution pipeline

Spatial decisions land before motion. Phase 2 never rescues a broken tree.

Phase 1

Spatial layout pass

  1. 01 Sketch the box family tree.
  2. 02 Set outer bounds at 1.5–2rem.
  3. 03 Split headers, controls, and actions.
  4. 04 Intra-cluster 0.5rem, inter-cluster 1.5rem.
  5. 05 Normalize sibling heights, often 2.5rem.
  6. 06 Distribute with space-between.
  7. 07 Fluid type and a grid with no breakpoint.

Phase 2

Kinetic interaction pass

  1. 08 Attach the motion tokens and curves.
  2. 09 Animate transform and opacity only.
  3. 10 Measure with getBoundingClientRect.
  4. 11 Stagger the sequence.
  5. 12 Audit the anti-pattern matrix.

06 Anti-pattern diagnostic matrix

Four failures you can drag or flip, then the full matrix.

Stepped type

Drag across 640, 768, and 1024. The left sample jumps. The right sample is clamp(1.5rem, 4vw, 3rem) against the same width.

Button bloat

Equal padding fights the shape of a word. The corrected control keeps the 2×–3× ratio from Law 2.

Layout thrash versus transform

The left box animates left. The right box animates translate3d. Replay both. Only the transform stays off the layout thread.

Flatland flip versus 3D

Without perspective, a turn is a horizontal squash. With perspective and preserved 3D, the card occupies depth.

Premature counter

The left badge changes on click. The right badge changes when the clone arrives. The cart should agree with the eye.

Full matrix

Defect Symptom Corrective action
Stepped breakpoint type The font snaps at a viewport threshold. Replace the stack with a clamp() tier.
Uniform spacing One gap everywhere. Hierarchy disappears. 0.5rem inside a cluster, 1.5rem between clusters, 2rem on the frame.
Symmetrical button bloat The control looks tall and square. Horizontal padding is 2×–3× the vertical padding.
Inner gap exceeds padding The frame stops holding the group. Keep the inner gap at or under the container padding.
Layout thrashing Animating top, left, width, height, or margin drops frames. Animate translate3d and opacity.
Flatland 3D flip The card squashes like a sticker. perspective on the scene, preserve-3d on the flipper, hidden backfaces.
Sticky flex stretch The sticky column scrolls away with the article. align-self: flex-start on the sticky item.
Flex content blowout A long token forces horizontal scroll. flex: 1 1 0% and min-width: 0 on the children.
Drawer shoving Opening the drawer crushes the content. Absolute on small screens, static in flow on wide screens.
Destructive projectile The grid cell leaves with the item. Clone, position fixed, animate the clone, remove it on finish.
Vector morph glitch The icon pops or tears. Match vertex count and command order before animating d.
