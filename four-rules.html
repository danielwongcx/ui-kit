Typography

Four rules 齊大分留

SOROE / OOKIKU / WAKERU / AKERU

Study after NHK E-TV Design Ah「ならべた」.

Typography study, not a news report. Public totals only: Urbtix 1.30 million, SOGO 0.82 million. The Before percent is a layout fault. October is set apart, with no invented count.

Rules

01

齊 Align SOROE

Top-align or baseline-align. Dates, labels, and numbers share one vertical line. A date on the left is not repeated on the right.

02

大 Enlarge OOKIKU

The one or two facts grow to 150–200%. Secondary type drops to about half. Here the counts are Urbtix 1.30 million and SOGO 0.82 million.

03

分 Separate WAKERU

Months, types, and levels of importance split by row, region, or color. October stays out of November. A percent stays out of a headcount. Use a line, space, or a color block.

04

留 Leave space AKERU

At least 0.5 em between rows, half the local character height. On a dark ground, open the line-height. If the title says November, the body does not say it again.

Checklist After

  • Duplicate date? Delete one.
  • Duplicate title? Delete one.
  • Mixed months in one cluster? Move them apart.
  • Percentage mixed with a headcount? Keep the count only.
